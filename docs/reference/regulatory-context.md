# Regulatory and Standards Context

## Scope and ownership

This document explains why the lifecycle is strict and points agents to authoritative sources. It is not a regulatory strategy, standards-applicability determination, legal opinion, or substitute for controlled company copies.

Regulatory/Quality owns device qualification, classification, conformity-assessment strategy, applicable requirements, standards/version selection, deviations, and approval. Agents must cite approved project decisions rather than making their own.

## Primary EU and German sources

- [Regulation (EU) 2017/745 on medical devices (MDR), consolidated text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/745/2024-07-09/eng)
  - Article 10: manufacturer obligations and QMS.
  - Annex I: general safety and performance requirements.
  - Annex II: technical documentation.
  - Annex III: post-market-surveillance technical documentation.
  - Annex XIV: clinical evaluation and post-market clinical follow-up.
- [Regulation (EU) 2017/746 on in vitro diagnostic medical devices (IVDR)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/746/oj/eng)
- [German Medizinprodukterecht-Durchführungsgesetz (MPDG)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/mpdg/)
- [German Medizinprodukte-Anwendermelde- und Informationsverordnung (MPAMIV)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/mpamiv/)
- [European Commission MDCG guidance index](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/new-regulations/guidance-mdcg-endorsed-documents-and-other-guidance_en)
- [Current EU MDR harmonised-standards publications](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-topics-interest/harmonised-standards_en)

Link to regulations rather than storing stale copies unless the QMS requires a controlled snapshot. Record the access/version date used for a regulatory decision.

## Common lifecycle standards

Applicability and exact editions must be determined and documented for each product.

- [ISO 13485 — Medical-device quality management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/59752.html)
- [ISO 14971 — Application of risk management to medical devices](https://www.iso.org/standard/72704.html)
- [IEC 62304 — Medical-device software lifecycle processes](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/22794)
- [IEC 62366-1 — Usability engineering for medical devices](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67220)
- [IEC 81001-5-1 — Security activities in the health-software lifecycle](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/63293)
- [IEC 60601-1 — Basic safety and essential performance of medical electrical equipment](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67497)

Standards are generally copyrighted. Do not copy their normative text into this repository. Use licensed, access-controlled company copies and cite the standard, edition, clause, and any applicable European amendment.

## Interpretation for agents

- Compliance comes from the complete QMS and product evidence, not from following this repository alone.
- A standard being widely used does not prove that it is applicable, harmonized, or the correct edition for a product.
- The latest international edition may differ from the version cited in the EU Official Journal.
- Regulatory and standards changes must enter the project through controlled regulatory-intelligence and change-management processes.
- When unsure whether a change affects a regulated artifact, stop and request Quality/Regulatory assessment.
